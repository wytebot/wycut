# WyCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

WyCut processes Google account identifiers, email, project titles, subtitle text, AI outputs, technical request data and payment/credit records to operate the service. Subtitle and narration text may be sent to configured AI providers, including Google, OpenAI or Deepgram. Video files are processed locally where supported and are uploaded to Google Drive only when you explicitly export them. Flutterwave processes payments; WyCut stores transaction references, amount, currency, status and credit records, not card PINs or OTPs.

You can disconnect Google permissions in your Google account. Contact the official WyCut support channel for data or billing requests. Add a monitored support email and business details before public launch. Provider retention and processing are also governed by their own policies.


### Free-trial abuse prevention
WyCut stores HMAC-SHA-256 hashes of trial device identifiers, normalized email addresses, and account identifiers to enforce one free recap per account and device. A daily aggregate counter limits free-trial volume. These hashes are used for eligibility and abuse prevention; raw device identifiers are not intended to be stored in trial-claim documents. Device identifiers can be reset or unavailable, so this signal is combined with account verification and is not treated as proof of a person's identity.
