# WyCut Acceptable Use Policy

Use only content you own or are authorized to process. Do not circumvent DRM or platform restrictions, infringe copyright, submit malware, harass or defraud people, bypass payment/usage controls, overload providers or interfere with other users. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information without a lawful basis. WyCut may restrict access to protect the service or comply with law. Report abuse or security issues through the official support channel.
