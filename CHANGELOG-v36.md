# WyCut v36 — Editing tools expansion

- Added 10 live-preview color filter presets: Original, Cinematic, Vivid, Mono, Warm, Cool, Vintage, Dream, Dramatic, and Fade.
- Added transition choices: Cut, Fade, Dissolve, Slide left/right, Zoom in/out, and Flash.
- Expanded sticker badges and emoji/sticker decoration options for recap-style videos.
- Added filter, transition, and sticker selections to the Drive `Project.json` settings and restores them when reopening a project.
- Filter is applied to the in-editor source preview. Transition and sticker selections are currently edit-plan/preview settings; the MP4 renderer has not yet been updated to bake filters, transitions, stickers, or overlay layers into final exports. The editor labels this limitation rather than implying export support.
- Added `scripts/audit-v36-editing-tools.mjs`.
