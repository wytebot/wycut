# WyCut Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Report a debit without confirmed credit, duplicate charge or missing credit restoration to WyCut support with the order reference, date and amount. Never send card PINs, OTPs or full card details. Failed script generation is designed to restore a reserved recap credit. Unused digital credits may be non-refundable except where required by law, duplicate/unauthorized payment occurs, or service delivery failed. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method; timing depends on the payment provider and bank.

Before live payments, add a monitored support email and align this policy with your merchant agreement and applicable consumer-protection law.
