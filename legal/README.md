# Standalone legal pages

The editable source documents for the app routes are in `app/privacy/page.tsx`, `app/terms/page.tsx`, `app/refunds/page.tsx`, and `app/acceptable-use/page.tsx`. Markdown copies are in this folder for convenient review and handoff.

These are starter policy templates, not legal advice. Before accepting live payments, add a monitored support contact and your business/jurisdiction details, then have the privacy, consumer-protection, copyright and refund terms reviewed for the markets where WyCut is offered.
